What we stand for. Three promises behind every product.

Quality and safety, first

We never compromise on quality. Every product meets strict international safety standards, and an independent quality-control company inspects each shipment before it leaves. That’s why fewer than 1 in 200 products comes back faulty.

Real value for money

You get what you pay for, so we make sure you get a lot. We’re not the cheapest on the shelf and we don’t try to be. We price for the quality and performance you’ll notice every day.

The essentials, done right

We focus on the accessories everyone needs: charging, power, cables, audio and car. Fewer products, made well, that work with the devices you already own.
